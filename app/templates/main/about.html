About Skill Exchange Platform

A collaborative network designed for non-monetary peer-to-peer knowledge sharing.

How It Works
  1. Create Your Profile: List the skills you are passionate about teaching and the subjects you want to learn.
  2. Discover Teachers: Search the marketplace by category, keyword, or user feedback score.
  3. Connect & Chat: Send a learning request. Once accepted, coordinate session details in real-time.
  4. Review & Build Reputation: Mark your session completed and exchange ratings to build trust.
Built with Integrity: All user feedback is verified per completed session. Soft deactivation preserves complete historical records.
